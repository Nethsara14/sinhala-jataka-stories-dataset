1. Overview

Sinhala Jataka Stories ("the App") is a free, offline-first educational and cultural mobile application providing access to 548 Sinhala Jataka Stories. Your privacy is fully respected.

2. Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The App does not require user registration, login, or account creation.
  • All story data is bundled locally within the application for offline reading.
  • User preferences (such as theme selection, font size, reading progress, and bookmarked favorites) are stored strictly on your local device using local storage and are never sent to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The App contains no advertisements, no analytics trackers, and no third-party data collection SDKs. External links (such as GitHub or developer website links in the "More" section) open in your device's default web browser.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults.
